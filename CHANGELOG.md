# Changelog — XP PLAY Member System

## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-16
### Added
- **Phase 0**: Audit menyeluruh project existing dan penyusunan arsitektur incremental.
- **Phase 1**: Backend Express (`server.ts`) + Firebase Provisioning + Auth Register & Login (Username/WhatsApp + Password & Google Sign-In) + Server-side CAPTCHA (Google reCAPTCHA & HMAC-signed SVG fallback).
- **Phase 2–4**: Member Dashboard mobile-first, pemisahan saldo `XP Progress` vs `XP Reward`, Dual Ledger (`xpProgressLedger`, `xpRewardLedger`), dan Referral berbasis username (`+10 XP` daftar, `+5 XP` referral).
- **Phase 5–8**: Transaction XP cabang `DEMANG` & `SEKIP` (integer `floor(amount / rate)`), Level dinamis (`Bronze`, `Silver`, `Juragan`, `Sultan`), katalog Benefit & Level Benefit, serta manajemen Challenge/Event/Promo dengan auto-expiration.
- **Phase 9–11**: Pengajuan Claim XP dengan upload bukti foto terproteksi (`ON_PROCESS` -> `APPROVED/DENIED`), Redemption XP Reward & Level Benefit atomik dan idempoten.
- **Phase 12–18**: Admin Console 19 menu, Member Management (soft status), Admin RBAC (`SUPER_ADMIN`, `ADMIN`, `STAFF`), Notification & Inactivity Campaign (`7/14/30/custom` hari), Non-destructive Idempotent Cycle Reset + Snapshot, Audit Logs, dan Reporting.
- **Phase 19–22**: Zero-Trust `firestore.rules` (lolos `@firebase/eslint-plugin-security-rules`), `storage.rules`, `firestore.indexes.json`, Vitest suite (`src/tests/xpSystem.test.ts`), dan dokumentasi lengkap (`README.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `DATABASE.md`, `AUTH.md`, `SECURITY.md`, `XP_SYSTEM.md`, `ADMIN_GUIDE.md`, `MEMBER_GUIDE.md`, `DEPLOYMENT.md`, `TESTING.md`).

- **Phase F & G — Full System Backup, 9-Check Verification, Retention & 9-Component System Health (`Backup & Restore`)**: `BackupRestoreEngine` dengan isolasi direktori `.backups/<backupId>/`, collision lock (`backupLock`), dukungan dual-mode (Admin SDK / `SUPER_ADMIN` Firestore REST), pelaporan scope jujur (`FULL_SYSTEM_BACKUP` vs `FIRESTORE_AND_STORAGE_BACKUP`), 9 pemeriksaan verifikasi otomatis sebelum `COMPLETED`, inspeksi `manifest.json`, kebijakan retensi dengan perlindungan backup sukses & pre-restore terbaru, serta panel kesehatan 9 komponen sistem.
- **Phase H & I — Full System Restore, Pre-Restore Backup, Restore Lock & Post-Restore Verification**: Alur restore khusus `SUPER_ADMIN` dengan `Preview Restore`, konfirmasi ketat `RESTORE XP PLAY` + alasan, aktivasi otomatis **Restore Lock / Maintenance Mode** (zero-PII `systemOperations/restoreLock`, middleware `requireSystemNotLocked()`, layar maintenance real-time di Member Portal), pembuatan **Pre-Restore Backup** wajib sebelum menimpa data, perlindungan permanen `auditLogs`/`systemBackups`/`restoreJobs`/`systemOperations`, verifikasi jumlah dokumen & storage pasca-restore, status ketat `VERIFICATION_FAILED` saat gagal, serta Force Unlock darurat.
- **Phase J — Hardened `firestore.rules`, Blueprint & Indexes**: Penegakan `!isSystemRestoreLocked()` pada seluruh mutasi operasional, pembatasan eksklusif `SUPER_ADMIN` pada `systemBackups`, `restoreJobs`, dan `systemOperations`, zero-PII enforcement (`data.lockedByEmail == null`) pada `restoreLock`, serta validator Eight Pillars yang lolos `@firebase/eslint-plugin-security-rules`.
- **Phase K — 12-Point Data Integrity Audit**: Engine `runDataIntegrityChecks` yang dijalankan otomatis pasca-restore dan dapat dipicu kapan saja secara on-demand (`POST /api/admin/integrity-audit`) mencakup ke-12 pemeriksaan konsistensi XP, Level, Unikitas Username/WhatsApp, Referral, Claims, Redemptions, Transaksi, Storage, dan Aset CMS.

## [3.0.0 — FASE 0–42 Unified Benefit Request, XP Hold, Cashier Mode & Profile Upgrade] — 2026-11-18
### Added
- **Unified Benefit Request & Immediate Atomic XP Hold (`benefitRequests`)**: Pengajuan Benefit Umum, Level Benefit, Challenge, Event, dan Promo disatukan dengan pemotongan saldo **XP Reward Tersedia -> Reserved XP Hold (`BENEFIT_REQUEST_HOLD`)** seketika di dalam transaksi atomik tanpa mengubah `XP Progress` maupun `Level`. Koleksi legacy `claims` dan `redemptions` tetap dipertahankan utuh.
- **Approval 7-Hari, Auto-Refund & Kebijakan `refundOnExpiry`**: Approval menetapkan masa berlaku 7 hari (`expiresAt = approvedAt + 7 hari`) dan melepaskan `reservedXp` permanen; penolakan (`DENY`) otomatis mengembalikan XP (`DENIED_REFUND`) dan mengunci status `REFUNDED`; proses expired melewati 7 hari mendukung toggle `systemSettings.refundOnExpiry` (`EXPIRED` atau `REFUNDED` + `EXPIRY_REFUND`).
- **Mode Kasir / Verifikasi Benefit (`AdminBenefitRequests.tsx`)**: Pencarian cepat berdasarkan Request ID (`BRQ-...`), Username, Nama, atau WhatsApp dengan validasi masa berlaku 7 hari dan aksi atomik **`MARK AS USED`** (cabang + nomor struk opsional + audit log + notifikasi member).
- **Profil Member Lengkap (Tanggal Lahir & Status Member)**: Validasi kalender Gregorian Asia/Jakarta (menolak tanggal masa depan & ekstraksi `birthMonth`/`birthDay` untuk kampanye ulang tahun) dan kategori `MAHASISWA | PELAJAR | PEKERJA | LAINNYA` pada registrasi, profil member, serta tambah/edit member Admin.
- **Dashboard Overview Member Baru, 8-Tab Member Detail Drawer & Filter Lanjutan**: KPI Member Baru (`Hari Ini / 7 Hari / 30 Hari`), panel `Member Baru Terdaftar` & `Notifikasi Member Baru (NEW_MEMBER_REGISTERED)` dengan tombol `View Member`, Drawer Detail Member 8 tab (`AdminMemberDetail.tsx`), filter/sorting member 10 opsi, filter transaksi preset tanggal Jakarta + custom range, serta tabel terpadu **Riwayat XP Ledger 12 kolom** (`AdminXpHistory.tsx`) dengan klasifikasi `+ / - / Hold / Refund`.
- **Streamlined Member Dashboard (4-Tab Mobile-First)**: Navigasi bersih (`Ringkasan`, `Benefit`, `Riwayat`, `Profil`) menampilkan `XP Reward Tersedia` + `Ditahan (Hold)`, voucher `BRQ-...` siap salin, countdown 7 hari, riwayat legacy collapsible, dan editor profil lengkap.
- **Backup Schema v3 (26 Koleksi) & 15-Point Data Integrity Audit**: Penambahan koleksi `benefitRequests` pada backup/restore (`CURRENT_APP_VERSION = '3.0.0'`, `CURRENT_SCHEMA_VERSION = 'xpplay-schema-v3'`) serta 3 cek integritas baru (`NO_ORPHAN_BENEFIT_REQUESTS`, `NO_DUPLICATE_DEDUCTION_OR_REFUND`, `VALID_MEMBER_STATUS_AND_PROFILE`).
- **Hardened `firestore.rules`, Composite Indexes, Vitest Suite & Docs**: Aturan Zero-Trust baru untuk `benefitRequests`, atomic `BENEFIT_REQUEST_HOLD`, `new_member_<uid>` admin alert, `refundOnExpiry`, `birthDate`/`memberCategory`; test suite baru `src/tests/benefitRequestAndProfiles.test.ts`; dokumentasi baru `XP_LEDGER.md` & `BENEFIT_REQUEST.md` serta pembaruan seluruh dokumen rujukan.

## [3.1.0 — Phases 1–38 Two-Step Membership Registration & Branch-Isolated Cashier RBAC] — 2026-11-19
### Added
- **Two-Step Membership Registration (Tahap 1 Kasir/Admin + Tahap 2 Customer Online)**:
  - **Tahap 1 Pre-Registration (`PRE_REGISTERED` / `PHONE_ONLY`)**: Input nomor WhatsApp + catatan opsional oleh Kasir (cabang terkunci `DEMANG`/`SEKIP`) atau Admin/Super Admin. Membuat profil `0 XP`, tanpa akun Firebase Auth, tanpa bonus `+10 XP`, tanpa referral, lengkap dengan validasi anti-duplikasi nomor WhatsApp.
  - **Cryptographic 6-Digit One-Time Verification Code (`onboardingSecurityService.ts`)**: Kode 6-digit bertanda tangan HMAC-SHA256 (`onboardingVerifications/{wHash}`), tidak pernah plaintext di publik, berlaku 7 hari, batas 5 percobaan salah, re-issue kode oleh Kasir/Admin, tautan QR `/register?wa=...&code=...`, dan `onboardingSessionToken` sekali pakai anti-replay.
  - **Tahap 2 Online Completion (`/register` & `/complete-membership`)**: Cek status zero-PII (`onboardingPublicStatus/{wHash}` & `POST /api/onboarding/check`), blokir mutlak nomor belum terdaftar, verifikasi 6-digit, lengkapi profil + password, aktivasi atomik Firebase Auth (`PRE_REGISTERED -> ACTIVE`), migrasi penuh transaksi/XP/benefit Tahap 1, **bonus `+10 XP` idempoten satu kali** (`regbonus_prog_<memberId>`, `regbonus_rew_<memberId>`), dan referral opsional `+5/+5 XP`.
  - **Proteksi Login `PRE_REGISTERED` & Operasi Tahap 1**: Blokir login `/member` bagi `PRE_REGISTERED` dengan CTA langsung ke `/register?wa=...`; dukungan penuh input Transaksi XP & Cashier-assisted Benefit Request bagi member `PRE_REGISTERED`.
- **Dedicated Branch-Isolated Cashier Portal (`/cashier`) & Formal 4-Role RBAC (`src/shared/rbac.ts`)**:
  - Portal khusus `CashierDashboardPage.tsx` dengan **hanya 9 menu resmi** yang terkunci ketat per cabang (`DEMANG` atau `SEKIP`), termasuk 4 slot kasir (`CASHIER_DEMANG_01/02`, `CASHIER_SEKIP_01/02`) tanpa kredensial hardcoded.
  - Pemisahan rute `/member`, `/cashier/*`, `/admin/*`, `/superadmin/*` dengan komponen **`403 UnauthorizedPage`** untuk direct-URL access dan pencatatan audit otomatis `CASHIER_DENIED_ACTION`.
  - Penegakan 4 lapis (`UI`, `Router 403`, `Express requirePermission & enforceBranchScopeInRequest`, `Firestore Rules isCashier() / cashierBranch() / isAdmin()`) yang memblokir Kasir dari XP Settings/Adjustment, Level Settings/Override, Password Reset Member, Benefit/Campaign CRUD, CMS, Admin/Cashier Management, Backup/Restore, dan data lintas cabang.
- **Admin Dashboard Split Metrics & Read-Only Incomplete Registration Report**:
  - Pemisahan metrik **`NEW PRE-REGISTERED`** vs **`NEW ACTIVE MEMBERS`** pada Admin Overview, panel Quick Pre-Register & filter `PRE_REGISTERED` pada Member Table, kartu status Two-Step + re-issue kode di `AdminMemberDetail.tsx`, serta laporan read-only **Pendaftaran Belum Selesai (7 / 14 / 30 hari)** di `AdminInactiveMembers.tsx` (tidak pernah auto-delete).
- **Hardened `firestore.rules` (Deployed), `storage.rules`, `firebase-blueprint.json`, `security_spec.md`, Tests & Docs**:
  - Blokir matematis registrasi publik bebas pada `members/{uid}`, isolasi cabang Kasir, validasi `isValidTransaction`, pembatasan baca `members/pre_*`, dan deny eksplisit `/cms/**` & `/backups/**` pada `storage.rules`.
  - Penambahan `src/tests/twoStepRegistrationAndCashier.test.ts` (24 tests mencakup seluruh **TEST 1 – TEST 20** + 4 test ekstra; total **80 / 80 tests pass**).
  - Penambahan dokumentasi baru `MEMBER_REGISTRATION.md`, `CASHIER_GUIDE.md`, `ROLE_PERMISSION.md` serta pembaruan `README.md`, `AUTH.md`, `DATABASE.md`, `SECURITY.md`, `ADMIN_GUIDE.md`, `XP_SYSTEM.md`, `TESTING.md`, dan `CHANGELOG.md`.
